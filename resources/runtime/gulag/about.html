USSR · GULAG

GULAG is the desktop editor for the USSR robotics project. Use it to inspect the 3D scene and organize a robot workcell.

Drag with the left mouse button to preview a selection. Right-click to open the scene menu.

Click a face, edge or corner of the ViewCube to orient the camera. Top and Bottom switch hemispheres. The wireframe cube on the left switches between orthographic and perspective projection.

Drag a panel by its title to dock or float it. Use Workspace → Panels to reopen a panel or restore the default layout.
